A strict gate must not false-fail on run-to-run noise: with a baseline rate recorded over N runs, a flat tolerance smaller than the rate's step (1/N) will flag normal variance. Use a variance-aware band (max(tolerance, 1/N)) and treat exactly one additional failed run vs. the baseline's expected failed count as a flake; a drop of more than one run is a regression. That makes the gate stricter against real regressions without false-failing on chance.
